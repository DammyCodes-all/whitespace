# Whitespace

Whitespace helps creators investigate which audiences their ideas might appeal to and where to begin reaching them. This glossary names the concepts used in the proposed audience-discovery redesign; current product behavior remains defined by `docs/product-spec.md`.

## Language

**Creator**:
The person making a creative work and choosing which audiences to investigate.
_Avoid_: Advertiser, audience member

**Pitch**:
The creator's description of the work they intend to make, which may not yet exist as a published cultural work.
_Avoid_: Qloo entity, finished product

**Pitch aspect**:
A distinct part of the idea, such as its premise, theme, tone, or form. A constraint or negation is part of the pitch but is not automatically a positive taste interest.
_Avoid_: Keyword, demographic segment

**Reference**:
An existing cultural work or entity used to represent a particular aspect of the pitch. Identifying a reference correctly does not establish that the analogy is appropriate.
_Avoid_: Proven audience, prediction

**Reference bridge**:
The explicit reason a reference represents a pitch aspect, together with what supports that analogy.
_Avoid_: Identity match, affinity score

**Reference lens**:
A view of one pitch aspect through a particular reference. Similar references or synonymous aspects do not automatically constitute different perspectives.
_Avoid_: Person profile, independent sample

**Supporting lens**:
An additional view of the pitch whose connection to an audience hypothesis provides supporting context. It does not constitute proof of demand or statistically independent validation.
_Avoid_: Ground truth, validation cohort

**Taste connection**:
A relationship between cultural interests supported by Qloo's aggregate taste data.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed preference, purchase intent

**Taste neighborhood**:
A coherent pattern among connected cultural works and interests. It is not a measured population of people or proof of a distinct community.
_Avoid_: Market segment, demographic cohort

**Audience hypothesis**:
A plausible audience to investigate, described through a taste neighborhood and its supporting evidence.
_Avoid_: Confirmed customer group, discovered demand

**Creative contrast**:
A distinction the creator wants to preserve between their idea and another work or experience. It does not imply that the intended audience dislikes that other work.
_Avoid_: Negative audience, dislike signal

**Evidence strength**:
The extent of the traceable taste connections supporting an audience hypothesis, alongside the aspects that remain unassessed.
_Avoid_: Probability of success, statistical confidence

**Investigation lead**:
A concrete entity or place to research when considering how to reach a proposed audience. A taste connection alone does not establish that the audience gathers there or that outreach is possible.
_Avoid_: Proven marketing channel, guaranteed reach
